How to prove internet outages to your ISP

“Everything works on our side” — the usual answer from support. To get the problem fixed, you need times, durations and proof that the problem is not your Wi-Fi. A phone can collect all of it.

1. Start the monitor

In NetWatch open More → Monitoring and tap Start monitoring. Allow notifications and background work — the monitor keeps running when the app is minimized. Keep the phone on Wi-Fi near the router and preferably on the charger.

2. Let it see where the problem is

Every 2 seconds NetWatch pings two things separately: your router and the internet.

  • Router doesn’t answer → the problem is inside your home: Wi-Fi, the router itself or its power.
  • Router answers, internet doesn’t → the problem is on the provider’s side. This is exactly what support needs to hear.

3. Leave it for a day or more

Each outage is recorded with its start time and duration, and you get a notification when the connection drops and when it comes back. The screen shows packet loss, the number of outages and total downtime.

4. Add speed tests

Outages are not the only problem — the internet can also be slow at certain hours. Run a few Speed tests in the morning and in the evening: History shows the average and how much slower it gets at different times of day.

5. Send the report

Tap More → Report → PDF. The report includes the monitor sessions with every outage, loss and downtime, speed tests, the Wi-Fi signal and network details. Attach it to your support ticket or send it in the chat with your provider.

What to write to support

Something like: “From 18:00 to 23:00 the internet dropped 7 times, 14 minutes in total. The router was reachable the whole time, so the problem is not my home network. Report attached.” Specific numbers get a much faster reaction than “it’s slow sometimes”.
